Dashboard design rationale (for PM annotation): Five KPIs at top are the daily headline numbers a manager glances at in under 10 seconds. Alert strip surfaces the most urgent action immediately on page load. The call log is live and ordered by recency — the manager can spot patterns (e.g., DNC cluster at a specific time) without clicking. Trust signals panel is given equal visual weight to conversion metrics because for Ditto, brand erosion is existential — not a secondary concern. The calling window heatmap gives the manager a data-driven basis for adjusting AI call schedules. Advisor queue integrates AI output with the human team — the AI's job ends when the advisor's begins, and the dashboard makes that handoff visible.
